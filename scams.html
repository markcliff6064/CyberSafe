Understand Online Scams. Protect Yourself.

Online scams are becoming increasingly sophisticated. Learn how scammers operate, recognize warning signs and protect your personal information.

KNOW THE THREATS

Common Types of Online Scams.

Understand the different ways scammers attempt to deceive people.

01

Phishing Scams

Fraudulent emails, messages or websites designed to trick people into revealing passwords, banking details or personal information.

Example: A fake bank message asking you to verify your account through an unfamiliar link.

02

Fake Investment Scams

Scammers promote fraudulent investment opportunities and promise unusually high returns with little or no risk.

Example: Someone promising to double your money within a few days.

03

Online Shopping Scams

Fraudulent sellers advertise products at attractive prices but may take payments without delivering the goods.

Example: A social media seller demanding full payment before disappearing.

04

Mobile Money Scams

Criminals use fake transaction messages, impersonation or social engineering to steal mobile money.

Example: Someone pretending to be customer support and requesting your mobile money PIN.

THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK

Warning Signs to Watch Out For.

Be cautious when you encounter these suspicious behaviours.

Unexpected Messages

Messages claiming you have won something you never entered.

Urgent Requests

Someone pressuring you to act immediately without thinking.

Requests for Sensitive Information

Requests for passwords, PINs, verification codes or banking details.

Unrealistic Promises

Offers that promise guaranteed profits or rewards that sound too good to be true.

TAKE ACTION

Think you've encountered a scam?

Stop communicating with the suspicious person. Do not send money or share personal information.

If you've shared financial information, contact your bank or mobile money provider through their official channels immediately.
