PROTECT YOUR ACCOUNTS

Your Passwords. Your First Defence.

Your passwords protect your digital identity. Learn how to create stronger passwords, avoid common mistakes and keep your online accounts secure.

BUILD STRONGER PASSWORDS

What Makes a Strong Password?

A strong password makes it harder for someone to gain unauthorized access to your accounts.

01

Use Long Passwords

Choose passwords that are long and difficult to guess. Longer passwords generally provide better protection.

02

Make Them Unique

Use a different password for each important account. Avoid reusing the same password across multiple websites.

03

Avoid Personal Information

Avoid using easily discovered information such as your name, birthday or phone number.

04

Use Passphrases

Consider using a long, memorable combination of unrelated words instead of a short predictable password.

AVOID THESE HABITS

Common Password Mistakes.

Small mistakes can put your online accounts at risk.

!

Reusing Passwords

Using one password for several accounts can expose multiple accounts if it is compromised.

!

Using Easy-to-Guess Passwords

Passwords such as common words, simple sequences or personal details are easier to guess.

!

Sharing Passwords

Sharing passwords through messages or with unauthorized people can compromise your accounts.

!

Ignoring Security Alerts

Ignoring unexpected sign-in notifications or account security warnings can leave threats unnoticed.

MANAGE YOUR PASSWORDS

What Is a Password Manager?

A password manager is a tool that securely stores and organizes your passwords. It can also help generate strong, unique passwords for your different accounts.

Why use one?

  • Store passwords in one secure place.
  • Generate strong and unique passwords.
  • Reduce the need to remember every password.
  • Make password management easier.
🔒

Keep Your Passwords Protected

Choose a reputable password manager and protect its master account with a strong password and additional security measures.

ADD AN EXTRA LAYER

Understanding Two-Factor Authentication.

Two-factor authentication adds another verification step when someone attempts to access your account.

01

First Verification

Enter your username and password to begin signing in.

→
02

Second Verification

Complete another verification step, such as an authenticator app approval or security key.

→
03

Account Access

After successful verification, you can access your account.

YOUR SECURITY CHECKLIST

Are Your Passwords Protected?

Review these important password security habits.

✓ I use long and unique passwords.
✓ I avoid reusing passwords.
✓ I use a reputable password manager.
✓ I enable two-factor authentication.
✓ I never share my passwords or verification codes.
